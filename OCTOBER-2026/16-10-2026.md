# LeetCode 2916 - Subarrays Distinct Element Sum of Squares II

## Problem

You are given a **0-indexed** integer array `nums`.

The **distinct count** of a subarray `nums[i..j]` is the number of distinct values in that subarray.

Return the **sum of the squares of distinct counts** of all subarrays of `nums`.

Since the answer may be very large, return it modulo `10^9 + 7`.

### Example 1

```text
Input: nums = [1,2,1]
Output: 15
Explanation:
Subarrays and their distinct counts:
[1]       -> 1
[2]       -> 1
[1]       -> 1
[1,2]     -> 2
[2,1]     -> 2
[1,2,1]   -> 2

Sum of squares = 1^2 + 1^2 + 1^2 + 2^2 + 2^2 + 2^2 = 15
